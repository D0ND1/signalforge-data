How we verify

1. We start from the source

Official pricing and plan pages, release notes, agency records, terms and reports from established outlets. Not rumours, and not other people's videos.

2. Every claim must match a quote

Before a video is made, each statement in the script is checked against a passage copied from the page it cites. A number in a video has to appear in that passage. A statement that can't be matched is dropped, not softened. The tables on this site are read from the same dated copies.

3. The source is on screen

Videos show the page they rely on, and the post and this site name it, so you can open it yourself. Pictures and clips, when used, are credited in the post.

4. A person approves every video

Nothing is posted until a person has watched it and approved it.

5. The voice is AI

The narration is a synthetic voice and is labelled as AI-generated on the platforms that ask for a label. We do not invent people, quotes or footage.

6. Things change

A video shows what the official page said on the day we checked. Prices and plans move, so check the source before you buy. If we find or are told of a mistake we fix or remove the video and log it on the corrections page.
