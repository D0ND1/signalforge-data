FDANOV 172026

Naia, Inc. Recalls Chocolate Banana Bar Sorbetto Because of Undeclared Milk, Eggs, and Nuts

Naia, Inc. of Hercules, CA is recalling Chocolate Banana Bar Sorbetto with code dates of 10/5/2027, 10/6/2027, 10/7/2027, and 10/8/2026, because it may contain undeclared tree nuts (pistachio), milk, and egg allergens. People who have an allergy or severe sensitivity to tree nuts (pistachio), milk or eggs run the risk of serious or life-threatening allergic reaction if they consume these products.

Published 2026-11-17 (the agency's date).

Larbi Inc. Recalls Yellow Dock Root Because of Possible Health Risk

Larbi Inc. of Valley Cottage, NY, is recalling three lots of Herb To Body Yellow Dock Root C/S 4oz, because it has the potential to be contaminated with Salmonella , an organism which can cause serious and sometimes fatal infections in young children, frail or elderly people, and others with weakened immune systems.
