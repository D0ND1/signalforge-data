Vehicles

  1. Find your VIN (vehicle identification number): it is on your registration, your insurance card and the driver's side of the dashboard.
  2. Enter it on NHTSA's recall lookup. It lists every open recall for that exact vehicle.
  3. If one is open, call a dealer of that brand and follow the remedy in the notice. If the notice says park outside or do not drive, do that until it is fixed.

Products

  1. Compare the product name, model number and dates in the official notice with the label on yours.
  2. If it matches, stop using it.
  3. Follow the remedy in the notice: it names who to contact for the refund, repair or replacement.

CPSC recalls

Food and medicine

  1. Check the brand, product name, lot code and dates on the package against the notice.
  2. If it matches, don't eat or use it. The notice says whether to return it or throw it away.
  3. If you are worried about your health, contact a doctor.
