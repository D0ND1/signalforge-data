# Privacy policy: SignalForge

Last updated: 2026-11-12. Operator: Real Ninjas LLC (Boston, MA, USA).

SignalForge is a private publishing tool run by its owner to post the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.
It is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it.

## What it accesses
- Google sign-in with the `youtube.upload` permission, used only to upload videos to the channel of the account that signed in.
- No email, contacts, files, or other Google data is requested or read.

## Storage
- The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer or server, in the operating system's secure key store.
- Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to third parties. No analytics or ads are attached to the sign-in.

## Removal
Revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Questions: open an issue in this repository.

SignalForge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
